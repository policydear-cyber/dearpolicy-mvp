TRIP CANCELLATION - WHAT IS COVERED
We pay your non-refundable travel and accommodation costs if you must cancel the trip
within 30 days before departure because of:
a) your death, serious injury or serious illness, or that of a close relative;
b) a natural disaster or outbreak of riot at the destination;
c) your home in Singapore being seriously damaged by fire or flood.
NOT COVERED
Change of mind or disinclination to travel. Events known before the trip was booked.
Failure by the travel agent or airline to provide the service (unless insolvency).
HOW TO CLAIM
Submit within 30 days of the cancellation with booking invoices, proof of
non-refund, and a medical certificate or official report supporting the reason.
