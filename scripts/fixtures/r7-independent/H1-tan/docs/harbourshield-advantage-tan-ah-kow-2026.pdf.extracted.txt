GENERAL EXCLUSIONS
We will not pay for:
1. Cosmetic surgery or treatment for beautification.
2. Dental treatment, except dental surgery needed because of an accident.
3. Spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids.
4. Pregnancy, childbirth and their complications.
5. Treatment received outside Singapore, except emergency overseas inpatient treatment.
6. Intentional self-inflicted injury.

SPECIFIC EXCLUSION ENDORSEMENT (applies to TAN AH KOW only)
Any treatment for osteoarthritis of the right knee, including total knee replacement,
is excluded from this policy and its rider.

HOW TO CLAIM
Ask the hospital to submit the claim electronically. Claims must be submitted
within 365 days from the date of discharge.
