Policy Wording (extract)
1. Definitions
1.1 Accident means a sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, violent and visible means,
occurring during the Period of Cover.
1.2 Confinement means admission as a registered in-patient in a licensed hospital in Singapore or overseas, on the
written advice of a registered medical practitioner.
2. Who is covered
2.1 The Insured Persons named in the Schedule. A Child is covered from 30 days of age until the policy anniversary
following his or her 18th birthday.
3. Scope of cover
3.1 Accident-only cover. All benefits in this Policy, including Items B and C, are payable only where the Confinement or
loss results solely and directly from an Accident and begins within 90 days of that Accident. Confinement due to illness or
disease is not covered.
4. Benefit conditions
4.1 Item B is payable for each day of Confinement up to the maximum number of days shown in the Schedule for each
Insured Person in each policy year.
4.2 How days of confinement are counted. Days are counted from the date and time of admission to the date and time
of discharge shown on the hospital record. Each complete period of 24 consecutive hours counts as one day. Any
remaining period of 12 hours or more counts as one further day; any remaining period of less than 12 hours is
disregarded. A Confinement of less than 12 hours in total is not payable. Days are not counted by calendar date or by the
number of nights.
4.3 Item C is payable only for the hours spent in an intensive care unit, counted in the same way as Clause 4.2.
4.4 For Items D and E, a Compound fracture means a fracture in which the broken bone breaks through the skin. All
other fractures are Simple fractures. Hairline fractures and stress fractures are treated as Simple fractures.
5. Exclusions
We will not pay any benefit for any loss, Confinement or expense directly or indirectly caused by or arising from:
5.1 intentional self-injury, suicide or attempted suicide, while sane or insane;
5.2 participation in professional sports, motor racing, scuba diving below 30 metres, sky-diving or mountaineering
requiring ropes or guides;
5.3 pregnancy, childbirth or miscarriage;
5.4 war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, civil war, rebellion, or nuclear, chemical or biological contamination;
5.5 the Insured Person being under the influence of alcohol above the legal driving limit or of drugs not prescribed by a
medical practitioner.
6. Claims
6.1 Written notice of a claim must be given within 30 days of the Accident. Claims must be supported by the hospital
discharge summary showing date and time of admission and discharge, original bills, and any medical certification
required by this Policy.
6.2 Claims may be submitted at meridianharbour.example/claims or by post to Claims Department, 8 Harbourfront Walk
#11-02, Singapore 098999.
