Waiting periods and exclusions
Waiting periods: 14 days for illness and 30 days for cruciate ligament conditions, from the start of the first period of
insurance. Accidents are covered immediately.
Pre-existing conditions are excluded. Declared at proposal: patellar luxation, left hind leg - excluded.
Also excluded: routine vaccinations, sterilisation, grooming, dental scaling, and breeding costs.
