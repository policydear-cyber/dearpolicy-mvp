FlexBen 2026 - How it works
Every confirmed employee receives 1,000 FlexPoints for the plan year 01 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2026. Use FlexPoints to
choose benefit options below. The options you actually chose are shown in your personal Enrolment Confirmation.
Unused FlexPoints are converted to your Wellness Spending Account at S$0.80 per point. Wellness claims (gym, health
screening, sports equipment) must be submitted by 31 Jan 2027.
If no election is made by the enrolment deadline of 05 Dec 2025, default options apply and all FlexPoints convert to the
Wellness Spending Account.
Elections are locked for the plan year. Changes are allowed only within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth
or adoption of a child).
All cover ends on the last day of employment.

FlexBen 2026 - Key terms
Group Hospital & Surgical: no deductible or co-insurance. Bills are settled with the hospital through a letter of guarantee,
up to the annual limit of the option chosen. Pre-existing conditions are covered after 12 months of continuous cover.
Group Critical Illness (CI1) applies only to employees who elected it.
Business Travel applies only to trips authorised by the company. Personal leisure travel is not covered.
Outpatient GP visits must be at a panel clinic; non-panel visits are not reimbursed.
