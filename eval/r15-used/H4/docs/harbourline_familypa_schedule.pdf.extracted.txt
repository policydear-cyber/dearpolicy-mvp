Eligibility and key exclusions
Children are covered up to age 18, or up to age 25 if in full-time education. Only persons named in this Schedule are
insured; other family members are not covered unless added by endorsement.
Exclusions include: professional sports; injuries while under the influence of alcohol or drugs; illness or disease of any
kind (this is an accident-only policy); pregnancy; war and terrorism.
Daily hospital income is payable from the first full day of hospital admission due to an accident, up to 30 days per
accident.
Claims must be notified within 30 days of the accident, with original bills and the medical report.
