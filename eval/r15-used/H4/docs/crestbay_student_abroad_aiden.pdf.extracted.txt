Important conditions
1. Coordination with OSHC: medical expenses recoverable under the Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) that the
Australian Government requires the student to hold are excluded. This plan pays the eligible balance not paid by OSHC,
after the excess.
2. Cover is suspended while the insured student is in Singapore.
3. Trips to countries other than Australia and Singapore during term breaks are covered for up to 21 days per trip.
4. Pre-existing medical conditions are not covered.
5. The insured must remain enrolled as a full-time student; cover ends 30 days after the student stops full-time study.
