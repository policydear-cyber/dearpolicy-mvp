General conditions
Pre-existing conditions are excluded unless declared to and accepted by Kestrel in writing.
Exclusions include cosmetic treatment, pregnancy and childbirth (except listed complications), dental treatment unless
needed because of an accident, and treatment outside Singapore unless it is an emergency.
Claims should be submitted within 90 days of discharge together with the final hospital bill.
This schedule must be read together with the policy contract and any endorsements issued.
