Claim rules
R1. A severe-stage claim for a condition will be paid less any early-stage or intermediate-stage amount already paid for
the same condition.
R2. Waiting period: no benefit is payable for Major Cancer, Heart Attack of Specified Severity, Coronary Artery By-pass
Surgery or Angioplasty if signs, symptoms or diagnosis occur within 90 days of the commencement date or reinstatement
date.
R3. Survival period: the life assured must survive 7 days after diagnosis.
R4. Between claims for unrelated conditions there must be at least 12 months between the dates of diagnosis. A
recurrence of the same cancer can be claimed only if 24 months have passed since the last cancer claim.
R5. Only conditions listed in the Condition Table (page 3) are covered. Any illness not listed, including infectious diseases
such as dengue fever, is not covered under this policy.
