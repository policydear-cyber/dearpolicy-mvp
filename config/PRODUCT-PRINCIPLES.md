# DearPolicy Product Principles

Users pay DearPolicy for certainty and action, not PDF storage.

DearPolicy should help a family answer four questions quickly:

1. **What do we actually have?** A clean household view across insurers and family members.
2. **What can we claim when something happens?** Guided fact-finding followed by policy-specific assessment and calculations.
3. **What do we need to do next?** A practical checklist of documents, deadlines and claim steps.
4. **Is our insurance still current?** Premium, renewal and stale-document reminders.

The paid value is reduced uncertainty, fewer missed claims, less time reading policy wording, a family-wide source of truth, and a clear evidence trail that can be used during stressful events.

The interface should therefore prioritise:

- evidence-specific portfolio status: confirmed cover, needs wording/review, or not established
- event-driven claims guidance
- evidence-backed calculations
- family member context
- currentness of policy/payment evidence
- minimal fact-finding questions
- no generic insurance education unless the user asks for it
- contextual conversation rather than fixed questionnaires
- open-ended scenario classification separated from policy eligibility
- independent assessment of each potentially relevant benefit
- visible potential payouts when arithmetic is supported, even while documentary conditions remain
- same-currency additive totals that exclude reimbursement estimates
- complete household review without a fixed policy-count ceiling
- measured review completeness, so a partial search is never presented as an absence of cover
- visible active-status evidence that stays separate from ownership
- visible analysis progress for local-model work
- deletion of one chat or selected/whole-account chat history without deleting policy documents
